Our 100-point framework

Business quality — 25: Competitive advantage, management, consistency and business durability.

Growth — 25: Revenue, profit and earnings growth; sector outlook.

Financial strength — 20: Debt, cash flow, capital efficiency and balance-sheet resilience.

Valuation — 20: P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, historical valuation and margin of safety.

Technical entry — 10: Trend, moving averages, momentum and risk/reward.

Bank-specific research

For banks, assess ROA, ROE, NIM, GNPA, NNPA, credit cost, CASA, capital adequacy, deposit growth and loan growth. Do not use industrial-company debt metrics blindly.

Score interpretation

80–100: Strong research candidate
65–79: Worth deeper research
50–64: Watch / improve evidence
Below 50: Low priority

A high score is not a buy signal. Valuation, risks and evidence must be independently verified.
