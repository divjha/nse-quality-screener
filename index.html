Source policy

Prefer NSE/BSE disclosures, audited reports, company investor-relations documents and permitted data feeds. Preserve reporting period, consolidated/standalone basis, units, source URL and verification date. Do not scrape exchange sites or bypass access controls.

Methodology

How we assess companies

Research score architecture

Fundamental proxy: up to 70 points from sector metrics, durability and governance. Valuation: up to 20 points. Technical entry: up to 10 points.

Current thresholds are indicative research heuristics, not universal truths. Compare peers, business models, accounting policies and cycles before making decisions.

Banks

ROA, ROE, NIM, GNPA, NNPA, credit cost, CASA, capital adequacy, loan/deposit growth. Also inspect slippages, provisions, liquidity and unsecured exposure.

NBFCs

AUM growth, spreads, cost of funds, GNPA/NNPA, credit cost, collections, ALM, capital adequacy and leverage.

IT services

Revenue growth, EBIT margin, profit growth, ROE, cash conversion and attrition.

Manufacturing

Revenue/profit growth, EBITDA margin, ROCE, debt/equity, CFO/PAT and interest coverage.

Insurance

Premium growth, VNB margin, persistency, solvency, combined ratio and ROE.

Consumer

Underlying volume growth, revenue growth, EBITDA margin, profit growth, ROCE, cash conversion, debt and working-capital days. Separate price/mix from volume.

Healthcare / pharma

Revenue and profit growth, EBITDA margin, ROCE, cash conversion, debt and R&D intensity. Track regulatory and product concentration risks separately.

Industrials

Revenue and profit growth, EBITDA margin, ROCE, cash conversion, debt, interest cover and order inflow growth. Review order book quality and working capital.

Other companies

Revenue/profit CAGR, margins, ROCE/ROE, cash flow, debt, interest cover and working-capital cycle.

Score vs confidence

A score is not confidence. Missing metrics lower completeness. A high score with weak or stale evidence must not be treated as a strong investment case.
